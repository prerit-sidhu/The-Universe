About Explore Planets

Explore Planets is a unique project aimed at providing a visual and interactive way to explore our solar system. Learn about each planet, its characteristics, and real-time data. Here we provide real-time distance of the planets only from Earth i.e the distance you are looking at, is the distance of the planet from the Earth. This website is still in it's development stage and we will be adding more features and planets in the future. Please feel free to contact us anytime and give your feedback and ideas to improve this website.

**Note:** The data presented on this website is not entirely accurate but is very close to accuracy. This site is intended for informational purposes only.
